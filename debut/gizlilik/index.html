Debut Football — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

This policy covers Debut Football (Android, package name com.msgames.debutfootball), developed by MSGames.

Data we collect

We do not collect any personal data. The game does not ask you to create an account and does not access your name, email, location, contacts, photos or device identifiers.

Game save

Your career progress is stored only on your own phone. It is not sent to us or anyone else. Uninstalling the game deletes the save.

Ads, analytics and third parties

The game contains no ads, no analytics tools and no in-app purchases. We do not share or sell your data to third parties.

Permissions

The game is played offline. The Android package includes an internet permission added by the game engine (Unity); the game does not send data using it. The vibration permission is used only for kick feedback.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. No personal data is collected from any age group.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
